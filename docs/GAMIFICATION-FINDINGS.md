# Gamification research: the findings ledger

The 56 findings behind [GAMIFICATION.md](GAMIFICATION.md), in four research angles. Each was found by one research agent and then checked by a second agent that opened the source and tried to refute it: 41 were confirmed as stated, 15 were corrected (a number, author, year or nuance), and none were dropped. Claims are summaries in our own words; follow the link for the source itself.

Strength (strong, moderate or weak) is the research agent's own rating of how solid each source's evidence is; there was no fixed rubric, so read it as a rough guide.
